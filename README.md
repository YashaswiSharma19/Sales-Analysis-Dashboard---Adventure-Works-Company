# Sales-Analysis-Dashboard---Adventure-Works-Company
## Description
This comprehensive project aims to analyse the sales of Adventure Works Company. The project makes use of best practices in Power BI to analyse the sales data of the company and to draw insightful information that contributes in raising the sales of the company.
key Business Metrics:
1. Order Quantity
2. Return Quality
3. Product Cost
4. Customer Annual Income
5. Discount Prize

Visualisation Views:

🔸 Thumbnail Dashboard: Depicting revenue trends using line graph. 
Bar chart to showcase orders category wise.
Top trend product matrix.
kPI cards for Revenue, Profit,Orders and Return rate.

🔸Map view: Map with slicer panels depicting regional orders

🔸 Product view: Detailed view of product metrics like profit and returns using line chart.
Gauge chart to track monthly orders, revenue and profit against the target value.

🔸 Customer view: Detailed view of customer depicting top customer by revenue.
Donut chart showcasing orders by customer income level and customer occupation.
Revenue per customer depicted through line chart.

## Generated Insigts
1. Revenue Trends:
-The company’s revenue exhibits seasonal fluctuations, with peak sales observed during the month of january.
-Certain product categories consistently outperform others, contributing the most to overall revenue.

2. Order and Return Analysis:
-High-volume orders are concentrated in a few top-selling categories.
-Return rates are higher for specific products like shorts, indicating potential quality issues or mismatches with customer expectations.

3. Product Performance:
-Top-performing products are tubes and tires. But top products in terms of profit are not always the highest in order quantity, highlighting the importance of focusing on high-margin items.

4. Customer Insights:
-High-revenue customers (Mr. Maurice Shan) tend to belong to higher income brackets, emphasizing the value of targeting premium segments.
-Occupation-specific purchasing patterns were observed, enabling more personalized marketing campaigns.

5. Regional Trends:
-Certain regions contribute exceptionally (like United States) to total orders and revenue, guiding potential focus areas for regional marketing or sales expansion.

6. Overall Recommendations:
-Focus marketing and promotions on high-performing products and premium customer segments.
-Investigate products with high return rates to reduce losses.
-Leverage regional trends to allocate resources effectively and target growth opportunities.
